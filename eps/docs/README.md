The files were renamed to this repository's lowercase-hyphen style; their
contents are unchanged. The two Google Drive files are the ones listed in the
[R-Massive index of Ensoniq technical documents](https://zine.r-massive.com/ensoniq-technical-documents-and-schematics/),
which also covers other models.

The classic EPS uses the ES5504 DOC II. ES5505 (OTIS) and ES5510 documents
describe the EPS-16 Plus and later machines and do not apply here.

Schematics, the service manual and other documents that are linked but not
hosted here are listed in the [EPS index](../README.md).
